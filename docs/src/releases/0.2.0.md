# 0.2.0

Prepared 2026-11-26. The wrap of milestone M2, *Guest Wi-Fi*, with milestone M1, *Skeleton*,
folded in: no 0.1.0 tag was made, because the exit of M1 waits for the Tier 1 device. For
development and testing; not for operators (ROADMAP, release cycle). The tag is the owner's
step after the device checks listed under *Open* below.

## What it contains

| Area | Content | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Claim on the setup Wi-Fi, the management Wi-Fi with its key, login with delay after wrong passwords, sessions in memory, Erase through a boot | 3, 4 |
| Guest Wi-Fi | The two modes `open_to_all` (OWE Transition Mode) and `encrypted_only` (OWE), the guest name, the notice page in every language of the UI | 7 |
| Separation | Two bridges with every port isolated, one DNS and DHCP instance per network bound to its address, no IPv6 on the Wi-Fi networks, the firewall rules of ENK-ISO-001 to 006, the listener on the management address only | 2 |
| Changes | The transaction: marker on flash, apply on its own thread, health check from the kernel's view, disconnection and confirmation by the next request, undo on failure, on the deadline and at start-up | 6 §4 |
| Effective state | Status from readings at most 2 s old; the two client counts; drift reported and never overwritten, *restore* as the ordinary change; adoption at start-up | 6, 7 |
| Topology | Uplink by DHCP; renumbering of the colliding Wi-Fi network only, never back; the daemon follows its management address through a procd restart | 2 |
| Platform | The package through procd as a non-root user with a ubusd ACL, port 80 redirected to the daemon's port, ownership of the four configuration files restored at every start, bridge-port isolation set by a hotplug script | 5, 6 |
| UI | Phone-first operator UI in English and Japanese, no external request, the word rules, the printable notice, the connection line | 10 |
| Build | MIPS build from the pinned SDK and nightly with the budgets enforced; the OpenWrt package; device profiles for the two Tier 1 routers and the VM | 8, 11 |

## Review against the security chapters (ENK-SCOPE-004, ENK-GUEST-002)

Every statement the device makes about protection was read against chapters 2, 7 and 12 §7 for
this release: the notice (`notice.p1`, `notice.p2`), the status counts (`status.counts.*`), the
mode lines (`mode.*.line`), the connection line (`connection.*`), the hardening line
(`status.hardening`) and the change details (`change.detail.*`). Each says what is observed
(configured, running, associated) and what the encryption covers (the radio link) and does not
(who runs the network, a fake network with the same name, what sites do). No colour, badge or
word sums protection up as good or bad. Nothing was found to change.

## Deviations to carry into the next amendment RFC

Port 80 redirected to the daemon's port 8080; the setup network on the management bridge; the
snapshot is the previous document; `validating` is never observable; ENK-CFG-025's port scan
read as the kernel's socket table; PMF not read back from hostapd; the radio hardware options
are not drift; renumbering never moves a network back; the daemon's own listener on the address
it is leaving is not "exposed" during the move.
